Online course

Biohacking Your Brain Hormones

A structured 4-week program that combines neuroscience with meditation, for calm, energy and focus. The course is in Hebrew.

  • 33 short lessons
  • 8 stages
  • 5–10 minutes each
  • Lifetime access
  • Full refund within a week

What are the 8 stages?

  1. Dopamine, the motivation hormoneHow to balance the hormone that drives action, in a few simple ways.
  2. Meditation from the ground upWhat meditation is, why it helps, how to sit, and starting to practice.
  3. Oxytocin, the connection hormoneHow touch and connection balance it, and how to feel connected.
  4. Connection meditationA simple technique to ground yourself even when thoughts flood in.
  5. Serotonin, the mood hormoneHow we disturb it without noticing, and how to balance it from morning to night.
  6. Objectless meditationA deeper, ancient practice once the basics are in place.
  7. Endorphins, nature’s calming drugsWays to release them and stay calm under outside pressure.
  8. Deep relaxation meditationOne of the most effective meditations for complete relaxation.

What do students say?

Nice to meet you

I’m Arik Leon Grinberg, a certified yoga therapist and a guest lecturer at the Cinematheque.

For more than 15 years I have been studying and practicing ways from yoga, Buddhism, therapy and brain research to feel better in body and mind in the Western world we live in.

What began as a search out of an unhappy life full of addictions became my life’s mission: to help you break free of invisible chains, reach your potential and live a fuller, calmer life.
